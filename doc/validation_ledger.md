# Detector validation ledger

_Last updated: v0.37.0_

Sleuth ships 20 built-in detectors. This ledger records, for each one, what evidence backs its thresholds and heuristics and where that evidence lives. Each row restates the detector's `validationMetadata` getter in `lib/src/detectors/`, which is the source of truth.

Two tests check the metadata and this ledger on every `fvm flutter test` run:

- [`detector_metadata_audit_test.dart`](../test/validation/detector_metadata_audit_test.dart) checks that every built-in detector has metadata with a non-empty rationale, that every tier above `unvalidated` names a reproducer file that exists, and that every `runtimeVerified` claim has three on-device captures that bracket its threshold. Anchor blocks pin each detector's reproducer path and stable ids, so a renamed file or a changed id fails the run.
- [`ledger_sync_test.dart`](../test/validation/ledger_sync_test.dart) checks that the tier counts in the tables below and in the summary line match the metadata.

## Evidence tiers

There are four tiers, from weakest to strongest. Each tier requires the evidence of the tier below it. The enum is frozen: adding a tier is a semver major change.

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `unvalidated` | The rationale is written down, but no hermetic reproducer or citation backs the thresholds yet. |
| `reproducerOnly` | A deterministic, hermetic test pins the detector's thresholds and stable-id families. It lives at `test/validation/<d>_reproducer_test.dart` when purpose-written, or at `test/detectors/<d>_detector_test.dart` when the unit tests already drive the emission path; every current detector uses the first. Structural reproducers run real `pumpWidget` and `scanTree`. VM reproducers may call `processTimelineData` or `processHeapSample` directly, or feed raw `List<TimelineEvent>` through `TimelineParser.parse()` into the detector. Both stay in this tier, but the parser path is stronger evidence. A raise to `runtimeVerified` needs profile-mode data from a real device. |
| `runtimeVerified` | `reproducerOnly` plus three checked-in profile-mode captures from a reference device, below, at and above the threshold. |
| `externallyCited` | `runtimeVerified` plus a citation to a Flutter SDK constant, a dart-lang issue, or a published benchmark. |

### Brackets

A detector's `tier` is its base tier, the minimum for every family it emits. `DetectorMetadata.perStableIdTier` raises single families above it; the effective tier of a family is `perStableIdTier[id] ?? tier`. A detector that emits one family may raise `tier` itself instead, as HeavyCompute and PlatformChannel do.

The evidence for a raise is a bracket: three captures whose measured magnitude falls below, at and above a threshold, checked by `ProfileCaptureSchema.validateBracket`. The top-level `bracket*` fields hold the canonical bracket. `additionalBrackets: List<BracketSpec>` adds one bracket for each further severity or measured axis of a family, each with its own threshold, captures and cross-check, and the audit validates every bracket the same way. A bracket's `observedAxisArgKey` names the `extraTraceArgs` value the detector stamps on each emission, so the audit compares the magnitude the operator recorded with the magnitude the detector measured. The audit keys bracket uniqueness on `(stableId, severityLabel, argKey)`.

### Reproducer strength

All reproducers sit in `reproducerOnly`, but they run different amounts of the production path:

1. VM detectors whose reproducers feed raw `List<TimelineEvent>` through `TimelineParser.parse()`: ShaderJank, HeavyCompute, PlatformChannel, and the hybrids GpuPressure, Repaint and Rebuild, which also mount real widget trees in the same file. MemoryPressure calls `processHeapSample` and `recordGcCycle` directly and names the upstream steps it skips. StreamResource feeds synthetic `AllocationProfile` responses through a fetcher seam.
2. The nine structural detectors drive real `pumpWidget` and `scanTree` on hermetic trees, most of them through the shared `test/validation/_helpers/structural_reproducer_harness.dart`. Startup injects `StartupMetrics` through `Sleuth.setStartupMetricsForTest`.
3. FrameTiming, NetworkMonitor and TrackedResource run their own entry points: `handleTimingsForTest` with real `FrameTiming` values, a loopback `HttpServer` behind `SleuthHttpOverrides`, and the `track` and `untrack` calls with an injected clock and a simulated finalizer.

VM reproducers share `test/validation/_helpers/vm_reproducer_harness.dart`, whose `parseAndAssertShape` asserts the parser produced the expected counts, so an event the parser drops fails the test. Underscore-parametric families (`repaint_debug_<typeName>`, `rebuild_debug_<typeName>`) are declared in `parametricFamilies`; the audit credits a literal `<family>_<suffix>` only with a non-empty suffix.

## How to contribute a tier raise

Pick a detector or stable-id family below the tier you want. There are two raise paths:
